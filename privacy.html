Microphone & speech recognition

LearnSpace requests microphone access **only** inside pronunciation practice (“Speak it” and **Record & compare** in a words session). There are two modes:

  • **Speech check (optional):** with your permission, your **device’s built-in speech recognition service** listens while your child says the word, and the app compares what was heard with the target word to show per-letter feedback. On many devices this runs fully on-device; on others, the platform’s speech service may process the audio using its own services (including cloud processing) **subject to Apple’s or Google’s own privacy policies**. LearnSpace does **not** receive, store, or transmit the audio or the transcript — the transcript is used in memory for scoring only and is not saved.
  • **Record & compare (fallback):** if no speech service is available, the app records audio **locally** so your child can listen back. Recordings are temporary files in the app cache and are deleted when you leave the sheet or start a new recording. They are never uploaded.

    • If a child pronounces a word correctly, the app awards in-app credits — computed on your device.

    You can deny microphone permission at any time; every other feature keeps working, and pronunciation falls back to record-and-compare or listen-only.

    Notifications

    LearnSpace offers an **optional daily learning reminder** you can enable in Settings. If you enable it:

  • The reminder is a **local notification** scheduled on your device. We do not run push notification servers and receive no delivery data.
  • Scheduling uses only your chosen time — no other data leaves the device.
  • You can turn reminders off at any time in Settings, which cancels the scheduled notification immediately.

    • Notification permission is requested only when you enable the reminder; denying it does not affect any other feature.

    Children

    LearnSpace is intended for learners ages **5+** with parental or educator guidance where appropriate. We do not knowingly collect personal information beyond what is stored locally on the device. There is **no social feed, open chat, or account registration** in the current app.

    If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided information you want removed, uninstalling the app clears locally stored data (or use **Reset progress** in Settings).

    Third-party services

  • **Device text-to-speech (TTS):** Provided by the operating system; subject to Apple/Google platform policies.
  • **Device speech recognition (optional pronunciation check):** Provided by the operating system (Google on Android, Apple on iOS). When the platform processes audio off-device, that handling is governed by Apple’s/Google’s own privacy policies — LearnSpace never sees the audio.
  • **App stores:** If you purchase **Full**, payment is processed by Apple App Store or Google Play; we receive purchase confirmation only, not payment card details.

    • We do not embed third-party advertising SDKs in LearnSpace.

    Data retention & deletion

    All progress data remains on your device until you:

  • tap **Reset progress** in Settings, or
  • uninstall the app.

    • Temporary pronunciation recordings are stored in the app cache and removed when superseded or when the compare sheet is closed.

    Security

    Data is stored using the platform’s standard app sandbox (e.g. SharedPreferences on Android/iOS). We do not transmit learner progress to LearnSpace servers in the current offline-first release.

    International users

    The app supports English and Arabic UI. Data stays on the device regardless of region.

    Changes to this policy

    We will update this page when we add features that change data practices (e.g. optional cloud sync or analytics). Material changes will be reflected in the app’s store listing and version notes.
